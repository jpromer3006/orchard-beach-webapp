The official facts

Source: NYC Parks, Orchard Beach & Promenade

Hours & swimming

During beach season, lifeguards are on duty daily from 10 AM to 6 PM. Swimming is prohibited when lifeguards are off duty and in closed sections, which are marked with signs and red flags. The official beach section map is updated each summer morning soon after 10 AM.

Parking, officially

In season: $10 on weekdays and $12 on weekends and holidays for cars and vans. Buses, trucks, and commercial vehicles pay $15 every day. Seniors 65+ pay $6 on weekdays with valid ID at the toll booths. Coupon booklet: 10 trips for $75 (weekdays) or $90 (weekends and holidays).

Accessibility

This is an accessible beach. Beach mats are available for wheelchair access, and beach wheelchairs are provided by reservation. Accessible ramps connect the pavilion, plaza, and sand.

Know before you go

Beach rules

A quick summary of the most important rules. The official and complete list is maintained by NYC Parks at the link below.

🏊

Swim with lifeguards only

Swimming is allowed only when lifeguards are on duty, 10 AM to 6 PM in season. Red flags mean no swimming.

🍺

No alcohol or glass

Alcoholic beverages and glass containers are not permitted on the beach or promenade.

🐕

No pets on the sand

Pets are not allowed on the beach or boardwalk. Service animals are always welcome.

🍳

Barbecue in marked areas

Grilling is allowed only in the designated picnic and barbecue areas, never on the sand.

🔊

Keep the volume neighborly

Amplified sound requires a permit. Be considerate in quiet areas.

🚩

Leave no trace

Use trash and recycling bins, and pack out what you pack in. Littering carries fines.

Safety (official)

  • Beaches are open Memorial Day weekend through the Sunday after Labor Day. Lifeguards daily 10 AM to 6 PM; swimming is prohibited when they are off duty and in closed sections.
  • Free SPF 30 sunscreen program at all eight NYC beaches; apply before you go and reapply often.
  • Water quality advisories (NYC Health)
  • Rip current safety (National Weather Service)

  • Barbecuing is allowed, in the North and South Picnic Areas.

Before you submit

Every submission is reviewed by Parks staff before anything is published. Please only send content you took yourself and have the right to share, with no identifiable people without their permission, and nothing inappropriate. Submissions that do not meet these guidelines are removed.
