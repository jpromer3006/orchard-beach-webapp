MAP + CONCIERGE PHOTOS (folder: images/locations/):

The interactive map, the location cards, and the itinerary slideshow all
look for one photo per location, matching the image_path values seeded in
sql/orchard_beach.sql:

Any photo that is missing shows a clean generated placeholder with the
location name, so the page and the slideshow never look broken.

Landscape photos around 1600 x 1000 pixels look best. JPG or PNG both work
(keep the .jpg name, or update image_path in the database via phpMyAdmin).

WHERE TO GET PHOTOS YOU CAN USE
-------------------------------
- Your own photos of the beach.
- Wikimedia Commons (search "Orchard Beach Bronx"). Many images there are
  freely licensed under Creative Commons. Check the license and give credit
  if required.
- Avoid copying photos straight from news sites or the official NYC Parks
  page, since those are usually copyrighted.
